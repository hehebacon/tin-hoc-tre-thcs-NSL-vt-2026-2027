RESCUE QUADRUPED — PRINT SPEC STATUS
====================================

STATUS: HOLD — NOT APPROVED FOR PRINTING

The old 800 mm / long-leg specification below belongs to a superseded concept and must not be used as the competition build specification.

Current project constraints:
- Four-legged quadruped, intended 12 logical joints if compatible actuators are available.
- New-purchase budget cap: 1,000,000 VND; see BUDGET_1000000.md.
- Exact controller board and exact servo model are not yet confirmed.
- Active firmware geometry in RobotConfig.h is coxa 35 mm, femur 65 mm, tibia 85 mm. CAD has not yet been verified against this geometry.
- CAD generator no longer globally scales the whole robot. If the authored assembly exceeds its target, the build must fail until the body/head/tail/leg placement is redesigned.

Do not print the old tracked STL files as final mechanical parts. They are legacy outputs and may be scaled or incompatible with the real hardware.

Before print approval:
1. Confirm the official arena size limit from the competition rules.
2. Confirm controller/servo models and measure their mounting interfaces.
3. Redesign the assembly to fit the real interfaces at full scale.
4. Run CAD generation and verify each bounding box and mounting clearance.
5. Print a single bracket / one leg test first.
6. Test fit with the actual servo and fasteners.
7. Only then print the complete frame.

Appearance is secondary to fit, mass, repairability, safe wiring and stable locomotion.
