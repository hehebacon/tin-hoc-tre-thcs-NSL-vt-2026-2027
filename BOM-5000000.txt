IMPORTANT POWER NOTE:
- Servo rail must be separated from the ESP32 logic rail.
- The UBEC feeds the servo rail.
- The buck feeds logic/display as appropriate.
- Do NOT power the 12 servos from the ESP32 or a small 5V regulator.
- Actual current demand must be tested before full-load operation.
