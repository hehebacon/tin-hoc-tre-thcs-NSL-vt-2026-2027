# XZORT Dragon Rescue Quadruped — STL preview modules

These are **visual concept meshes** for checking the dragon silhouette and module placement. They are not validated, load-bearing, servo-fit, or print-ready parts.

## Files
- `head_shell.stl` — stylized head/muzzle/horn silhouette
- `back_armor.stl` — dorsal armor plates
- `tail_shell.stl` — curved segmented tail concept
- `leg_covers.stl` — four simplified leg-cover silhouettes
- `assembly_preview.stl` — combined visual preview

Units: millimetres. Overall concept envelope targets approximately 350 mm. STL does not retain colors. Before printing, replace the concept meshes with dimensioned CAD and verify servo mounts, wall thickness, joints, clearances, balance, and assembly.
