XZORT RESCUE QUADRUPED — CURRENT BUILD STATUS
================================================

Concept: four-legged dragon/lizard-style robot; target 12 logical servo channels.
Current firmware baseline: ESP32/Arduino C++ + PCA9685 driver abstraction.
IMPORTANT: Huna Robo compatibility is not verified. A board supporting C/C++ is not automatically compatible with ESP32-specific APIs. Confirm exact board model, SDK/toolchain and competition rules before porting or purchasing.

CURRENT CONSTRAINTS
- New-purchase ceiling: 1,000,000 VND; read BUDGET_1000000.md.
- The old 5,000,000 VND BOM is superseded.
- No display, thermal camera, environment sensors or online AI hardware in the minimum build unless required by the actual task.
- Existing/borrowed school hardware must be inventoried before purchasing.
- Do not claim a complete reliable 12-servo robot can be bought from zero within 1M VND.
- The exact competition arena size limit must be confirmed from official rules; 350 mm is only a project target, not yet verified as an official rule.

FIRMWARE GEOMETRY SOURCE OF TRUTH
- Coxa 35 mm
- Femur 65 mm
- Tibia 85 mm
Defined in RobotConfig.h. Legacy documents with 45/75/105 mm or 800 mm overall dimensions are not the current mechanical specification.

CAD STATUS
- Global scaling is disabled because it invalidates servo housings, holes, clearances and linkage lengths.
- Current CAD design is expected to be blocked while oversized; this is intentional until the geometry is redesigned at full scale.
- Tracked STL/STEP files are legacy artifacts and are NOT approved for printing.

SAFE BUILD ORDER
1. Inventory exact board, servos, power, wiring and fabrication access.
2. Confirm controller is permitted by competition rules.
3. Test one servo and the PWM path with hardware outputs disabled by default.
4. Build and test one complete 3-DOF leg.
5. Reconcile CAD dimensions with the real servo and firmware geometry.
6. Add the other legs only when budget and parts are confirmed.
7. Prove stand, slow gait, turns and mission behavior in stages.
8. Add only task-required sensors after the locomotion base is reliable.
