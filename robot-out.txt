 ├── Metal servo horns
 ├── Steel shafts
 ├── Bearings
 ├── Aluminum load-bearing frame
 └── PETG/nylon 3D-printed outer shell


===============================================================
                       DESIGN LANGUAGE
===============================================================

Shape:
    futuristic rescue robot / robotic dog / spider-like
    compact body
    four articulated legs
    aggressive but friendly engineering appearance

Colors:
    BLACK       = main body
    BLUE        = accents / status lighting
    WHITE       = labels / small details

Materials:
    aluminum internal frame
    PETG/nylon outer shell
    metal joints / shafts / servo horns

Important:
    DO NOT make the legs look like simple V-shaped sticks.
    Each leg has a real 3-joint quadruped structure:
        COXA → FEMUR → TIBIA → FOOT

    The body should be relatively low and compact.
    The four legs extend from the four corners of the body.
    Front sensor module sits at the front.
    Touchscreen sits on the top of the body.
    Robot should look like a serious search-and-rescue prototype,
    not a toy.
